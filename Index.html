About the masjid

A place to pray, learn,
and belong.

Founded by families in the neighbourhood who wanted a nearby place to pray together, our masjid has grown into a full community centre — open for all five daily prayers, Jumu'ah, and the events that keep us connected between them.

We're run entirely by volunteers and supported by the community we serve. Whether you're here for salah, a class, or just somewhere quiet to sit, you're welcome.

5xDaily congregational prayers
200+Families in our community
15Years serving the area

Visiting for the first time?

Wudu facilities and shoe racks are at the main entrance. A separate, fully enclosed prayer space is available for sisters. Schools and community groups are welcome to arrange a tour.

Ramadan at the masjid

Nightly iftar and taraweeh throughout the month — overflow rooms open once the main hall fills.
